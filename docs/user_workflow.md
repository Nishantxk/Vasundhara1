# User Workflow Manual: Donating Waste, Tracking Points & Purchasing Products

## Introduction
Vasundhara connects urban sustainability advocates with rural manufacturing collectives. This manual outlines the exact end-to-end journey for citizens participating in the circular ecosystem.

---

## Part 1: Managing Your Profile & Green Points

### 1.1 Viewing Points Balance
At the top-left corner of the Global Header, your current verified **Approved Points** are visible alongside your user icon and full name.
- **Approved Points**: Verified credits eligible for direct financial deductions at checkout (1 Point = 1 INR).
- **Pending Points**: Credits queued from recent waste collection requests awaiting administrative physical verification.

### 1.2 Accessing the Profile Drawer
1. Click on your profile chip in the top-left header.
2. The side modal slides out, displaying:
   - Your registered Name, Phone Number, Email, and Primary Pickup/Delivery Address.
   - Points breakdown (Approved vs Pending).
   - An "Edit Profile" mode allowing you to update contact coordinates.
3. Save edits; updates immediately synchronize across the entire application and persist in local storage.

## Part 2: Donating Waste ("Be a Part")

### 2.1 Exploring Reward Exchange Rates
Scroll down to **Section 2: Be a Part** or click the **"Be a Part"** quick-card in the Hero section.
Review the live exchange tariff matrix:
- **Pre-Loved Traditional Sarees**: 40 Points per piece.
- **Clean Segregated Post-Consumer Plastic**: 15 Points per kilogram.
- **Worn Slippers & Footwear**: 20 Points per pair.

### 2.2 Submitting the Smart Pickup Form
1. Click the primary action button: **[ Take a Step ]**.
2. The **Smart Waste Donation Form** opens:
   - **Auto-Population**: Your Name, Address, Phone, and Email are automatically filled from your active UserProfile.
   - **Waste Category**: Select from the dropdown:
     - Sarees (Handcrafted Textiles)
     - Plastic Polymer (Bottles, Containers, Packaging)
     - Slippers & Footwear (Rubber/EVA)
   - **Quantity / Weight**: Enter the count or weight in kilograms. Notice the live estimated point counter dynamically updating in real-time.
   - **Pickup Schedule**: Select your preferred collection date and time window (Morning: 09:00 - 12:00 or Afternoon: 14:00 - 17:00).
   - **Special Instructions**: Optionally add landmark details or gate codes.
3. Click **[ Confirm & Schedule Pickup ]**.
4. **Immediate System Response**:
   - The system appends the calculated reward to your `pendingPoints` balance.
   - A success banner confirms: *"Items in process. Points pending admin approval."*
   - A logistics tracking identifier (e.g. `DON-2026-091`) is registered.

## Part 3: Purchasing Upcycled Products ("Products Catalog")

### 3.1 Browsing the Eco-Catalog
Navigate to **Section 1: Products** (or click the **"Upcycled Products"** hero card).
Explore authentic, artisan-crafted items created using reclaimed materials:
- **Upcycled Saree Multi-Tone Tote Bag** (Reclaims 2 discarded sarees)
- **Recycled Polymer Geometric Planter Set** (Reclaims 1.8 kg plastic)
- **Recycled EVA Footwear Acoustic Wall Tile** (Reclaims 6 slipper pairs)
- **Braided Heritage Saree Floor Runner** (Reclaims 4 discarded sarees)

Each product card displays high-resolution imagery, pricing in INR, artisan cluster attribution, and the ecological impact metric (kilograms or units diverted).

### 3.2 Product Customization Flow
- If you desire a customized item made from your own specific household scrap (e.g., custom sizes, bespoke color combinations from your family's vintage sarees), click **[ Customize Your Product ]** on any card.
- You will be immediately redirected to **Section 2: Be a Part**, with the donation form context pre-primed to initiate custom waste material intake.

### 3.3 Buying a Product with Points Discount
1. Click **[ Buy Now ]** on any product card.
2. The **Secure Checkout Modal** appears with:
   - Selected Product Details, Retail Base Price (INR), and Artisan Attribution.
   - Delivery Address confirmation (prefilled from your profile).
   - **Reward Points Redemption Panel**:
     - Shows your current Approved Points balance.
     - An interactive discount toggle/slider allowing you to redeem all or a custom portion of your approved points.
     - Dynamic Price Breakdown:
       - Base Retail Price: `₹[Price]`
       - Green Points Applied: `-₹[Redeemed Points]`
       - Logistics & Packaging: `FREE (Eco-Courier)`
       - **Total Final Payable**: `₹[Final Payable]`
3. Select your simulated payment gateway (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Cash on Delivery).
4. Click **[ Complete Sustainable Order ]**.
5. The order is confirmed, your `approvedPoints` balance is debited accordingly, and an official order receipt is generated.

## Part 4: Philanthropic Support ("Give a Funding")
1. Click **[ Give a Funding ]** in the top-right header navigation.
2. Review the Vasundhara Philanthropic Vision, executive governance pledges, and cluster expenditure transparency report.
3. Access direct enterprise bank transfer credentials and scan the high-resolution UPI / Paytm QR code to disburse seed grants directly to rural artisan toolkits.
